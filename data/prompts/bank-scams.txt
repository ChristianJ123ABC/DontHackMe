INTRO:
You will write practice questions for my app, DontHackMe, a website that teaches people with little to no technical background on how to spot  and avoid cyber threats

TASK:
Write EXACTLY 5 multiple-choice questions at <<DIFFICULTY>> difficulty. Each question gives a fictional scenario (Email, text message, phone call, social media message or a pop-up) and then asks what the person should notice or do.

WHAT EACH DIFFICULTY MEANS:
easy: The warning signs of an attacker are blatantly obvious (spelling mistakes, unknown sender, threatens or rushes the victim)
medium: Some warning signs are obvious but some are subtle
hard: The message seems believable and only gives one or two small details to show it's true intentions.
Only write the questions at the difficulty's above

RULES / GUIDELINES:
1. Everything generated must be fictional and safe. Never include real malware, threats, a real attack or anything that could be copied and used on a real person.
2. URLs and email domains must end in .example or use example.com or example.org (e.g. alert.example.com / login.example). Never use a real website
3. Never use a real company, shop, bank or brand name. Use a made-up name instead
4. Never create a real card number, phone number or account number. Do not write any of these numbers with 7 or more digits to ensure they're fake.
5. Use only the information in the MODULE TEXT below. Do not add advice that is not in it
6. Every question must have EXACTLY 4 options and EXACTLY 1 correct option.
7. scenario_type must be one of the following: email, sms, call, social, popup. Use a mix of these types
8. Keep the scenario under 800 characters, the question under 300, each option under 200 and the explanation under 600. 
9. The explanation says why the correct option is right and which warning sign the person should have noticed.
10. reference_id is the id number of the reference in the REFERENCES list that supports the answer the best. Use only an id from that list.
