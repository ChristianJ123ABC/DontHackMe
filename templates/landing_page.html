DontHackMe: Keep away cyber attacks today

Cybersecurity awareness app that guides you through real world online threats and how to detect and avoid them


The Problem

As cyberthreats and forms of AI-generated scams are becoming more common, users are more susceptible to attacks than ever.


How the app works

Module Education

You will be educated on a module to learn how to detect and avoid a threat.


Testing

At the end of each module, you will be provided a scenario generated by an AI to test your knowledge.


Results

After you complete a test, you will get your results displayed on a graph in your dashboard. This is used to motivate you to improve and for the AI to adjust the difficulty.


Features

The features include: Modules, Virus URL Scanner, Adaptive Scenarios generated by Artificial Intelligence, Progress charts.



User Security

Security is a crucial element of this app, and it will provide the following:

  • Two Factor Authentication
  • Hashed Passwords
  • Form inputs such as Register and Login, will be rate limited (limited attempts before temporarily stopping you).


Getting Started

Click the Register button to create an account, which will log you into the dashboard where you can start your cybersecurity journey!
